# Free Asset Candidates (statues & architecture)

**Purpose.** These packs could replace the procedural statues and kitbash landmarks with authored meshes. All entries are **free**.

**Status.** Researched 2026-10-31. Fab's catalogue changes, so **confirm the price shows "Free" on Fab before claiming.**

**Downloads.** Claiming and downloading needs your Epic account, through Fab in the browser or the Fab plugin in the editor. Once a pack is in `Content/`, the recipes can swap to it without touching gameplay.

## Recommended

| Pack | Publisher | Why it fits | Free status | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Infinity Blade: Grass Lands** | Epic Games | Stone citadel set pieces and large statues. Use for the Titan colossus, Sunscar colossi, Verdant guardians and Quarry gate. | Released free in 2015 as part of the Infinity Blade Collection; reported still free in 2019. **Current Fab listing not confirmed.** | Unreal Engine projects only |
| **Infinity Blade: Ice Lands** | Epic Games | Wintry fort in a glacial enclave. Use for Frostbound walls, towers and gates. | Same collection, same caveat | Unreal Engine only |
| **Infinity Blade: Fire Lands** | Epic Games | Castle interior with lava, dramatic props and braziers. Use for Ashfall. | Same collection, same caveat | Unreal Engine only |

The project already contains **InfinityBladeEffects** from the same collection, which is a good sign the collection is in your Epic library.
